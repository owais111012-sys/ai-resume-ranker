How to Customize These Prompts for Your Own Company

When using an automated resume screening engine, vague phrases like "Looking for a rockstar developer who loves fast-paced environments" add noise rather than signal. Instead, structure your requirement prompt into three explicit lines:

  1. Target Role & Minimum Experience: State the exact number of years (e.g., 3+ Years Experience) so the tenure parser can weight experience accurately.
  2. Comma-Separated Mandatory Skills: List 6 to 12 concrete technologies, frameworks, or certifications.
  3. Core Responsibilities: Summarize the primary business problem the hire will solve in 1 to 2 sentences.
