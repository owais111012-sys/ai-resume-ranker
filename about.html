About Our Platform

Making High-Volume Technical Hiring Faster, Transparent & Accessible

AI Resume Ranker was built to solve a practical problem faced by HR managers, startup founders, and technical recruiters worldwide: enterprise Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) often cost thousands of dollars per year, leaving small-to-medium recruitment teams to manually read hundreds of PDF and Word resumes one by one.

Our Core Principles

1. 100% Free Access

No credit cards, paywalls, or mandatory account signups. Recruiters can screen batches of 10 to 100+ resumes immediately.

2. Privacy by Design

Document text extraction happens locally in your browser using PDF.js and Mammoth.js. We never store or archive candidate files.

3. Explainable Scoring

Every candidate score includes transparent matched skills, missing skill gaps, and a clear summary rather than a "black box" number.
