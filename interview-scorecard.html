How to Interview Your Top 10 Shortlisted Candidates Using a Structured Rubric

Turn your AI-generated Top 10 shortlist into confident, bias-free hiring decisions.

Shortlisting the Top 10 deserving candidates out of 100 uploaded resumes saves your recruitment team hours of initial screening—but what happens next determines whether you make a great hire. Industrial-organizational psychology studies consistently demonstrate that unstructured interviews ("Tell me about yourself") are poor predictors of on-the-job performance. By pairing automated resume screening with a Structured Interview Scorecard, HR teams can evaluate every finalist on identical criteria.

1. The 1-to-5 Competency Grading Scale

During each 30-to-45 minute interview with your Top 10 finalists, interviewers should grade the candidate from 1 to 5 across four core pillars:

Score Rating Label Behavioral Evidence Observed in Interview
5 / 5 Exceptional / Bar Raiser Explains system trade-offs clearly, provides metrics-backed production examples, and demonstrates deep mastery of matched skills.
4 / 5 Strong Hire Meets all core technical requirements independently with minor coaching needed on secondary tools.
3 / 5 Mixed / Borderline Understands theoretical concepts listed on the resume but struggles to explain real-world debugging or architecture decisions.
1–2 / 5 Do Not Proceed Cannot substantiate the skills listed on the resume or lacks fundamental role prerequisites.

2. How to Probe the "Missing Skills / Gaps" Flagged by AI

When you run a batch of resumes through AI Resume Ranker, each candidate card lists both Matched Skills (in green) and Missing Skills / Gaps (in red). Rather than disqualifying an 85%-match candidate who is missing a single tool (such as Docker or GraphQL), use the first 10 minutes of the phone screen to ask targeted bridge questions:

  • "Your resume shows strong experience with PostgreSQL and Node.js, which matched our core stack. While Redis wasn't listed on your CV, have you worked with any in-memory caching layers or message queues in production?"
  • "Tell me about the most complex production outage or performance bottleneck you diagnosed in your last role. How did you isolate the root cause?"
  • "Walk me through a project where you had to learn an unfamiliar framework or cloud service under a tight deadline."

3. Combining ATS Match Scores with Interview Scores

For the highest hiring accuracy, export your Top 10 shortlist to Excel using the Export Shortlist to Excel (CSV) button on our main tool, and add three columns to the spreadsheet: Technical Depth (1–5), Problem Solving & Debugging (1–5), and Communication & Ownership (1–5). Comparing the initial AI resume match score alongside live human interview scores gives your hiring panel a complete, auditable decision record.
